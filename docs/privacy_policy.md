# Privacy Policy for Block Drop

**Effective Date:** November 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 10, 2026

Block Drop ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as an offline falling-block puzzle game for Android devices. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our players.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Block Drop does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device identifiers, locations, or usage analytics.
- **No Personal Data:** The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or accounts.
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not include Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or any tracking SDKs.
- **No Advertisements:** Block Drop is 100% ad-free and contains no advertising networks or trackers.

### 2. Device Storage and Local Preferences
The app uses standard Android local storage (`SharedPreferences`) strictly on your local device to store:
- Your game high scores and split times
- Your gameplay statistics (lines cleared, games played, combos)
- Your customized settings (theme, volume levels, haptic toggles, control sensitivity)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled or when you tap "Reset All Statistics" in the game settings.

### 3. Permissions
The app requests only the following minimal standard Android permission:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback during piece rotation, movement, and line clears. This can be toggled off at any time in the in-game Settings.
No internet or storage access permissions are requested.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Block Drop does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The game complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.
